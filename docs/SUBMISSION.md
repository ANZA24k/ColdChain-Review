Name: ColdChainReview
Summary: Evidence-grounded shipment temperature compliance reviews with GenLayer consensus and tamper-evident receipts.

ColdChainReview resolves handling-policy disputes between logistics parties. It locks agreed limits, ordered logs, and incident reports. Deterministic checks detect hard breaches and coverage gaps; independent GenLayer validators interpret permitted excursions using full readings and exact supporting quotations. Parties can challenge once and reassess, retaining prior verdicts in hash-linked receipts. The app supports public reads, browser-wallet transactions, a Studio inbuilt-wallet workflow, finality checks, and verified JSON exports. It does not authenticate sensors or certify product safety.
